## v0.1.0-dev

### 🚀 New components 🚀

- `browser`: Add the `browser.console`, `browser.navigation`, `browser.navigation_timing` and `browser.resource_timing` events and their `browser.*` attributes. ([#14](https://github.com/open-telemetry/semantic-conventions-client-side/issues/14))
- `enduser`: Add `enduser.id` and `enduser.pseudo.id`, moved from the core semantic conventions with their types, examples and PII warnings unchanged from core v1.44.0. ([#33](https://github.com/open-telemetry/semantic-conventions-client-side/issues/33))
  Core deprecates them in favor of this registry in
  [open-telemetry/semantic-conventions#4238](https://github.com/open-telemetry/semantic-conventions/pull/4238).
  The already-deprecated `enduser.role` and `enduser.scope` stay in core.
